About

15+ years of experience successfully leading enterprise IT projects — from the energy sector to the financial industry.

Andras Simon

IT Project Manager & AI Integrator

With 20+ years of industry and 15+ years of IT project management experience, I lead enterprise programs. Throughout my career, I have helped organizations such as MVM Ügyfélszolgálati Zrt. (DÉMÁSZ), E.ON Tiszántúli, OPUS TITÁSZ, MOL Nyrt., Allianz Hungária, and PPF Hungária deliver complex SAP IS-U migration, cloud transition, carve-out, data migration, and Workday rollout programs successfully.

I believe that a successful project is not just about technology — it is about people, communication, and strategic planning. I take on every project with the goal of creating real, measurable business value for my clients.

My Approach

The principles I follow in every project

Structured Planning

Every project starts with thorough planning. Risk assessment, milestones, and clear responsibilities defined from day one.

Transparent Communication

Regular status reports, proactive risk signaling, and open dialogue with all stakeholders. No surprises throughout the project.

Technology + Business

Technology decisions are always made through the lens of business goals and ROI. AI and automation are tools, not goals in themselves.
